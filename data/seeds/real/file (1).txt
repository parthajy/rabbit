**Date:** April 7, 2026
**Time:** 11:00 AM – 1:05 PM
**Location:** Hybrid (Bank HQ + Zoom)

**Participants (Client – Zenith National Bank):**

* Rajiv Malhotra (Chief Marketing Officer)

### **Meeting Begins**

**Rajiv Malhotra (CMO):**
Let’s not waste time. It’s April 7 today. The original contract signed on January 12, 2026 clearly stated Phase 1 delivery by March 15. We are now 23 days overdue. This is unacceptable.

---

**Aditya Verma (Creative Director):**
Rajiv, I understand the concern, but the delay isn’t unilateral. We had 11 revision cycles from your team between February 20 and March 28. That wasn’t part of the original scope.

---

**Sneha Iyer (VP Digital):**
Because your initial designs didn’t meet digital compliance standards. The February 18 version failed accessibility checks—contrast ratios, typography hierarchy, everything.

---

**Karan Bedi (Lead Designer):**
That’s not accurate. The February 18 version passed WCAG AA compliance. Your team introduced new requirements on March 2—AA+ standards—which were never in the brief.

---

**Vivek Suri (Retail Banking):**
Look, customers don’t care about your internal definitions. Our mobile app redesign was supposed to go live April 1. We missed that window.

---

**Laura Fernandes (Project Manager):**
Let’s align on facts. Timeline recap:

* Jan 12: Contract signed
* Jan 20: Discovery workshop
* Feb 10: First design submission
* Feb 18: Revised submission
* March 2: New compliance requirements added
* March 15: Original deadline
* March 28: Last feedback received

We’re not ignoring delays—but scope changed significantly.

---

**Amit Khanna (Procurement):**
Scope changes don’t justify missing contractual obligations without formal change orders. There were none.

---

**Meera Joshi (Account Director):**
We flagged it multiple times. Emails on March 3, March 9, and March 16 explicitly stated timeline risks.

---

**Rajiv:**
Emails are not change orders. Contracts are.

---

**Aditya:**
So you’re saying your team can double the work without adjusting timelines or budget?

---

**Amit:**
I’m saying the process wasn’t followed.

---

*(Tension rises, voices overlap briefly)*

---

**Rajiv:**
Enough. Let’s reset. We need three things today:

1. A new delivery timeline
2. Clarity on additional costs
3. Assurance this won’t slip again

---

**Sneha:**

**Karan:**

**Vivek:**

**Aditya:**

**Vivek:**

**Rajiv:**

**Aditya:**

**Decision 1:**
Split delivery into two phases:

* Phase 1A: April 15, 2026
* Phase 1B: April 22, 2026

---

**Action Item 1:** Laura to share detailed deliverables list for Phase 1A by April 8, 2026.

**Amit:**

**Rajiv:**

₹20 lakh.

**Meera:**

**Vivek:**

**Aditya:**
₹30 lakh is the lowest we can go.

---

**Amit:**
₹25 lakh, final.

**Aditya:**

---

**Rajiv:**
Done.
