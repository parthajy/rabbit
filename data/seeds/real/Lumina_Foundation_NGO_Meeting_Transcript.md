## BACKGROUND NOTE *(for record purposes)*

Lumina Education Foundation awarded Teach Forward Africa a three-year grant of **USD 2,400,000** under the *Expanding Quality Education in Rural East Africa* program. Grant period: **October 1, 2022 – September 30, 2025**. This meeting was convened on **February 3, 2025** following Lumina's review of Teach Forward Africa's Milestone 4 Progress Report, submitted on **January 20, 2025**, which indicated significant shortfalls against agreed indicators. Tranche 3 disbursement — originally scheduled for **November 15, 2024** — has been withheld pending this review.
